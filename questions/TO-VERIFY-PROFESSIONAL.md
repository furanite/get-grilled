# Finance, law, design and language questions to double-check

These questions were added in November 2026 to the new groups:
Finance & Economics (7 categories), Law (8), Architecture & Design (8) and Languages & Humanities (Urdu, Turkish, Farsi).
The facts below are the ones most likely to change over time, or that sources disagree on, so a specialist
should check them first. Fix or delete the line in the matching `questions/*.txt` file if it is wrong.

Who should check what:
- Islamic finance and fiqh: a scholar of fiqh al-mu'amalat (all rulings, school positions and dates).
- Saudi law, labour, commercial and criminal law: a Saudi-licensed lawyer or law faculty member.
- Finance and economics: accounting/finance faculty.
- Architecture, Islamic architecture and design: architecture faculty.
- Urdu, Turkish and Farsi: a fluent speaker of each (spelling of romanised words, proverbs, poets).

## Honorifics (all files)
- A single Companion carries ﵁ (﵂ for a woman). Several Companions named together carry "رضوان الله عليهم" written out, never ﵃. The honorifics test enforces this.
- Scholars and Muslim poets carry ﵀. Living people and rulers carry none, except Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz ﵀ (and Salah al-Din ﵀ and Nur al-Din Zangi ﵀ in Islamic Architecture). Please check that every ﵀ is for someone who has passed away.
- Writers without ﵀ on purpose: Uthman Taha (Madinah Mushaf calligrapher; add ﵀ if he has passed away), Munshi Premchand (not Muslim), Bahadur Shah Zafar (ruler).

## Islamic Finance (islamicfin.txt) and Fiqh (fiqh.txt)
- AAOIFI standard numbers and the years of IIFA (Islamic Fiqh Academy) resolutions: bonds ruling 1990, lease-to-own ruling 2000.
- Every "Halal or not?" item and every case-file ruling: check the stated school positions (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali) and the majority view.
- fiqh tf: a woman travelling for Hajj in a safe group without a mahram, Shafi'i position.
- fiqh 500: diyah for a woman reported from Umar ﵁ and Ali ﵁.
- criminal 300/500: the wine-drinking hadd of 40 and 80 lashes; Ibn Abbas ﵁ on hirabah.
- islamicfin: the waqf of Uthman ibn Affan ﵁ in Madinah (palm farms and a hotel).
- Grand Mufti dates (Ibn Baz ﵀ from 1993; Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ﵀ as the first).

## Saudi law (saudilaw.txt, complaw.txt, contracts.txt, criminal.txt, labour.txt, intlaw.txt)
- Dates, royal decree numbers and article numbers, especially: the Civil Transactions Law (2023), the Law of Evidence, the Personal Status Law (2022) and the Commercial Register Law (2024).
- Labour Law amendments that took effect in 2025 (notice periods, leave days, probation).
- GOSI contribution rates after the 2024 reform.
- Labour case-file sums: end-of-service award, Article 77 compensation, and the injury pay of 12,375 riyals. Noura's award is 18,667 riyals.
- Diyah amounts set in 2011 (300,000 / 400,000 riyals).
- Competition Law notification thresholds; the 50% mandatory offer rule in the CMA takeover rules.

## Finance (acct.txt, corpfin.txt, invest.txt, banking.txt, econ.txt, personalfin.txt)
- SAMA rates and limits, Tadawul and TASI facts, and VAT at 15% (since July 2020).
- Index and market figures that change every year.

## Farsi (farsi.txt)

- The share of Persian words that come from Arabic (about 40%).
- Word origins: English paradise, candy, lilac, jasmine, scarlet; Arabic jawhar, sirdab, banafsaj.
