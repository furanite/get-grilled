# Praise of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ in Persian and the Turkic languages of Central Asia. Only titles are quoted. Dates: hijri first, then CE.

## 100
The Persian word for a poem praising our Beloved Prophet ﷺ, used across Iran, Central Asia and South Asia. :: Na't || H: Standard reference
The Persian word for a poem praising Allah, which usually comes before the na't in a long poem. :: Hamd || H: Standard reference
The great Persian poem of Mawlana Jalal al-Din Rumi رحمة الله عليه, which opens with the lament of a reed flute. :: The Mathnawi || H: The Mathnawi
The city in Anatolia where Mawlana Jalal al-Din Rumi رحمة الله عليه lived, taught and is buried. :: Konya || H: Standard history
The Persian poet of Shiraz who wrote the Gulistan and the Bustan. :: Sa'di رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard history
The Persian poet of Herat, a Naqshbandi scholar, called "the seal of the poets". :: Mawlana 'Abd al-Rahman Jami رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard history
The Persian poet of Nishapur who wrote "Mantiq al-Tayr" (The Conference of the Birds). :: Farid al-Din 'Attar رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard history
The Turkic Sufi poet of Yasi whose "Diwan-i Hikmat" was sung across Central Asia. :: Khwajah Ahmad Yasawi رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard history
The city in Kazakhstan, once called Yasi, where Ahmad Yasawi's رحمة الله عليه great tomb stands. :: Turkistan || H: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Mausoleum of Khoja Ahmed Yasawi
The short sayings in verse of Ahmad Yasawi رحمة الله عليه are called this. :: Hikmat (wisdoms) || H: Diwan-i Hikmat

## 200
The poet of Herat who wrote in Chagatai Turkic and was a friend and student of Jami رحمة الله عليه. :: 'Ali-Shir Nava'i رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard history
The city, now in Afghanistan, where Jami and Nava'i رحمة الله عليهما lived. :: Herat || H: Standard history
The Persian poet of Ghazni whose "Hadiqat al-Haqiqah" set the model for Sufi mathnawis. :: Sana'i رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard history
The poet of Ganja whose five long poems are called the "Khamsa". :: Nizami Ganjawi رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard history
Many long Persian poems open with hamd, then na't, then a passage on this blessed night of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ. :: The Mi'raj (Night Journey and Ascension) || H: Makhzan al-Asrar (Nizami)
The first of Nizami's رحمة الله عليه five poems, full of praise and counsel. :: Makhzan al-Asrar || H: Makhzan al-Asrar
Rumi's رحمة الله عليه collection of ghazals is named after his spiritual companion. :: Diwan-i Shams-i Tabrizi (Diwan-i Kabir) || H: Standard reference
The Persian poem of Jami رحمة الله عليه, beginning "Nasima jaanib-e Batha guzar kun", asks this wind to carry greetings. :: The morning breeze (nasim) || H: Diwan-i Jami
The Persian word for a poem in praise of a saint or of the Blessed Family (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِمْ). :: Manqabat || H: Standard reference
The Sufi way of Jami رحمة الله عليه and of Baha' al-Din Naqshband رحمة الله عليه of Bukhara. :: The Naqshbandi way || H: Standard history

Nava'i رحمة الله عليه passed away in this year. :: 906 AH / 1501 CE || H: Standard history
Ahmad Yasawi رحمة الله عليه passed away in this year, by the common report. :: 562 AH / 1166 CE || H: Standard history
The ruler who ordered the great tomb of Ahmad Yasawi رحمة الله عليه in Turkistan. :: Timur || H: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Mausoleum of Khoja Ahmed Yasawi
Jami's رحمة الله عليه book of the lives of the Sufi saints. :: Nafahat al-Uns || H: Nafahat al-Uns
Jami's رحمة الله عليه seven long poems are gathered under this name. :: Haft Awrang (Seven Thrones) || H: Haft Awrang
Nava'i رحمة الله عليه wrote his own "Khamsa" in this language. :: Chagatai Turkic || H: Standard history
The Persian poem form in rhyming couplets that gave Mawlana Rumi's رحمة الله عليه great poem its name. :: Mathnawi (masnavi) || H: Standard reference

## 400

The famous Arabic quatrain on our Beloved Prophet ﷺ beginning "Balagha al-'ula bi-kamalihi" is credited to this poet. :: Sa'di رحمة الله عليه (by the common attribution) || H?: Standard attribution; not in all old copies of his works

Nizami رحمة الله عليه passed away in about this year. :: 605 AH / 1209 CE || H?: Standard history; dates differ
The Uzbek poet Sufi Allahyar رحمة الله عليه wrote this Turkic book of counsel and faith, read in Central Asian schools. :: Sabat al-'Ajizin || H: Standard reference
The father of Mawlana Rumi رحمة الله عليه, a scholar called "Sultan al-'Ulama'". :: Baha' al-Din Walad رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard history
The father of Rumi رحمة الله عليه wrote this book of sermons and visions. :: al-Ma'arif || H: Standard reference
The Mevlevi order of Rumi رحمة الله عليه was organised by his son. :: Sultan Walad رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard history
The mathnawi of Jami رحمة الله عليه on the story of our master Yusuf ﵇ and Zulaykha, named after them in Persian. :: "Yusuf u Zulaykha" (our master Yusuf ﵇ and Zulaykha) || H: Haft Awrang
Jami's رحمة الله عليه "Tuhfat al-Ahrar" follows the model of this poem by Nizami رحمة الله عليه. :: Makhzan al-Asrar || H: Haft Awrang
