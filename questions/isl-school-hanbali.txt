# The Hanbali madhhab: its imams, books, terms and well-known rulings. Every ruling here is the school's relied-upon (mu'tamad) view.
# Every Sunni scholar's name carries رحمة الله عليه. Dates: hijri first, then CE.

Imam Ahmad رحمة الله عليه stood firm in the trial (mihnah) over this question. :: Whether the Qur'an is created (he held it is the uncreated Speech of Allah) || H: Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' (al-Dhahabi)
The Hanbali scholar who wrote the great book al-Mughni. :: Ibn Qudamah al-Maqdisi رحمة الله عليه || H: al-Mughni

Imam Ahmad رحمة الله عليه, who stood firm in the trial, is called the Imam of these people. :: Ahl al-Sunnah (the People of the Sunnah) || H: Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' (al-Dhahabi)
In the Hanbali school, the tashahhud said in prayer is the one taught by this Companion. :: Our master 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud ﵁ || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
Shaykh 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani رحمة الله عليه, a Hanbali, is buried in this city. :: Baghdad || H: Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' (al-Dhahabi)

## 300
The Hanbali book Zad al-Mustaqni' was written by this scholar. :: al-Hajjawi رحمة الله عليه || H: Zad al-Mustaqni'
The Hanbali scholar al-Buhuti رحمة الله عليه wrote this commentary on Zad al-Mustaqni'. :: al-Rawd al-Murbi' || H: al-Rawd al-Murbi'
The Hanbali scholar al-Buhuti رحمة الله عليه wrote this large commentary on al-Iqna'. :: Kashshaf al-Qina' || H: Kashshaf al-Qina'
The Hanbali scholar of Baghdad, preacher and historian, who wrote Sayd al-Khatir and Daf' Shubah al-Tashbih. :: Ibn al-Jawzi رحمة الله عليه || H: Sayd al-Khatir
The Hanbali judge who wrote al-Ahkam al-Sultaniyyah, like al-Mawardi رحمة الله عليه. :: al-Qadi Abu Ya'la al-Farra' رحمة الله عليه || H: al-Ahkam al-Sultaniyyah (Abu Ya'la)
In the Hanbali school, the extra takbirs of Eid in the first rak'ah number this many, after the opening supplication. :: 6 || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, the extra takbirs of Eid in the second rak'ah number this many. :: 5 || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, the qunut of Witr is said at this point. :: After rising from bowing || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, water less than this amount becomes impure when najasah falls in, even with no change. :: Two qullahs || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, something touched by a dog or pig is washed this many times, once with earth. :: 7 || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, Jumu'ah needs at least this many men who live in the town. :: 40 || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, two prayers may be joined because of this, besides travel and rain. :: Illness (sickness that makes each on time hard) || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, the longest monthly period is this. :: 15 days || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, the shortest monthly period is this. :: A day and a night || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, shortening the prayer on a journey has this ruling. :: Better than praying in full (sunnah) || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)

## 400

In the Hanbali school, gold jewellery a woman wears for permitted use has this ruling for zakat. :: No zakat due || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, zakat al-fitr is given in this form. :: Food (dates, barley, wheat, raisins or dried yogurt) || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, a marriage contract needs this person on the bride's side. :: A guardian (wali) || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, the most rak'ahs of Witr is this number. :: 11 || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
The Hanbali scholar al-Mardawi رحمة الله عليه wrote this book showing the strongest view on each question. :: al-Insaf || H: al-Insaf

The Hanbali book Muntaha al-Iradat was written by this scholar. :: Ibn al-Najjar al-Futuhi رحمة الله عليه || H: Muntaha al-Iradat

Ibn Qudamah رحمة الله عليه wrote this mid-level Hanbali book, between 'Umdat al-Fiqh and al-Kafi. :: al-Muqni' || H: al-Muqni'
The Hanbali scholar Mar'i al-Karmi رحمة الله عليه wrote this short book for students. :: Dalil al-Talib || H: Dalil al-Talib
In the Hanbali school, prayer in the resting places of camels has this ruling. :: Not valid || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, the youngest age at which bleeding counts as a monthly period is this. :: 9 years || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
In the Hanbali school, the age after which bleeding is no longer a monthly period is this. :: 50 years || H: Zad al-Mustaqni' (al-Hajjawi)
The Hanbali scholar Ibn Qudamah رحمة الله عليه wrote this book on usul al-fiqh. :: Rawdat al-Nazir || H: Rawdat al-Nazir
