# Sunni scholars of the last century and today. Those who have passed away carry رحمة الله عليه (رحمة الله عليها for a woman); living scholars carry حفظه الله.
# No Wahhabi, Deobandi, Muslim Brotherhood or sectarian figures. Dates: hijri first, then CE.

## 100
The Egyptian scholar whose television talks on the Qur'an, "Khawatir", made him loved across the Arab world. :: Shaykh Muhammad Mutawalli al-Sha'rawi رحمة الله عليه || H: Tafsir al-Sha'rawi (Khawatir)
The Syrian scholar of Damascus, author of "Fiqh al-Sirah", who was martyred while teaching in a mosque in 1434 AH / 2013 CE. :: Shaykh Muhammad Sa'id Ramadan al-Buti رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard biography
The Tunisian scholar whose great tafsir is called "al-Tahrir wa-l-Tanwir". :: Shaykh Muhammad al-Tahir ibn 'Ashur رحمة الله عليه || H: al-Tahrir wa-l-Tanwir
The Syrian scholar whose many-volume work "al-Fiqh al-Islami wa-Adillatuh" sets out the views of the schools. :: Shaykh Wahbah al-Zuhayli رحمة الله عليه || H: al-Fiqh al-Islami wa-Adillatuh
The scholar of Makkah al-Mukarramah, of the family of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ, who wrote "Mafahim Yajibu an Tusahhah". :: Sayyid Muhammad ibn 'Alawi al-Maliki رحمة الله عليه || H: Mafahim Yajibu an Tusahhah
The Kurdish scholar of Turkey whose many-part tafsir and teaching are called the "Risale-i Nur". :: Bediüzzaman Sa'id Nursi رحمة الله عليه || H: Risale-i Nur Külliyatı
The scholar of Tarim in Yemen who founded the school Dar al-Mustafa. :: Habib 'Umar ibn Hafiz حفظه الله || H: Standard biography
The Egyptian scholar who has been Shaykh al-Azhar since 1431 AH / 2010 CE. :: Shaykh Ahmad al-Tayyib حفظه الله || H: Standard biography (al-Azhar)
The Egyptian scholar who was Grand Mufti of Egypt from 1424 AH / 2003 CE to 1434 AH / 2013 CE. :: Shaykh 'Ali Jum'ah حفظه الله || H: Standard biography (Dar al-Ifta' al-Misriyyah)
The last Shaykh al-Islam of the Ottoman state, who passed away in Cairo. :: Shaykh Mustafa Sabri رحمة الله عليه || H: al-A'lam (al-Zirikli)
The Ottoman scholar, deputy of the last Shaykh al-Islam, famous for his defence of the Hanafi school and of Ash'ari and Maturidi creed. :: Shaykh Muhammad Zahid al-Kawthari رحمة الله عليه || H: al-A'lam (al-Zirikli)
The Palestinian-born judge and poet who gathered "Jami' Karamat al-Awliya'" and al-Majmu'ah al-Nabhaniyyah. :: Shaykh Yusuf al-Nabhani رحمة الله عليه || H: al-A'lam (al-Zirikli)
The Grand Mufti of Egypt whose short tafsir "Safwat al-Bayan" is printed in the margin of many mushafs. :: Shaykh Hasanayn Muhammad Makhluf رحمة الله عليه || H: Safwat al-Bayan li-Ma'ani al-Qur'an
The Shaykh al-Azhar known as "the Ghazali of this age", who wrote on tasawwuf and the Blessed Seerah. :: Shaykh 'Abd al-Halim Mahmud رحمة الله عليه || H: Standard biography (al-Azhar)
The Mauritanian scholar of Maliki fiqh and usul who leads the Forum for Promoting Peace in Muslim Societies. :: Shaykh 'Abdullah ibn Bayyah حفظه الله || H: Standard biography
The Yemeni preacher of Tarim who founded the Tabah Foundation in Abu Dhabi. :: Habib 'Ali al-Jifri حفظه الله || H: Standard biography (Tabah Foundation)
