## case
A worker earning 6,000 riyals a month is let go after 4 years, not for misconduct. What end-of-service award is due? :: 12,000 riyals (half a month per year for the first five years)
A worker earning 10,000 riyals a month leaves after 10 years when the employer ends the contract. End-of-service award? :: 75,000 riyals (5 x 5,000 + 5 x 10,000)
A tenant stops paying rent on a registered Ejar contract. Must the landlord file a full lawsuit first? :: No; the Ejar contract is an enforceable instrument, so the enforcement court can act
A shopper receives an unopened jacket ordered online and changes her mind on day 5. Can she cancel? :: Yes; the E-Commerce Law allows cancelling within seven days if unused (with some exceptions)
A man posts a neighbour's private family photo on social media to embarrass him. What law is broken? :: The Anti-Cyber Crime Law (invasion of privacy)
Two firms' contract sends disputes to arbitration. One firm sues in the commercial court, and the other objects at once. What should the court do? :: Decline the case and send it to arbitration
Someone claims he lent a friend 150,000 riyals with only two witnesses and nothing written. What does the Law of Evidence say? :: Amounts above 100,000 riyals generally must be proven in writing
A company collects visitors' ID numbers on its website and sells them. Which authority can fine it? :: SDAIA (under the Personal Data Protection Law)
A shop owner lets an expatriate run the shop under his commercial registration and takes a monthly fee. What is the offence? :: Commercial concealment (tasattur); both can be punished
A Saudi woman of 23 wants to travel abroad alone. Does she need her guardian's permission? :: No (since the 2019 reforms, women over 21 can travel without it)
