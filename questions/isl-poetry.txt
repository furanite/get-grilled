The prince of Aleppo for whom al-Mutanabbi wrote many of his finest odes. :: Sayf al-Dawlah || H: Diwan al-Mutanabbi

A poem of satire is called this in Arabic. :: Hija' || H: al-'Umdah (Ibn Rashiq)

## 300
